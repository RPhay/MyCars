# Car review sites

Dedicated directory of where car *reviews* specifically get written (and filmed), as distinct from `car-sites.md` (search/listings/dealer ratings) and `general-dealership-review-sites.md` (dealership reputation). Some sites are already listed in `car-sites.md` for other reasons — repeated here because this file's job is to be the complete review-source list on its own.

|---|---|---|

|---|---|
| [Doug DeMuro](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCsqjHFMB_JYTaEnf_vmTNqg) | 5M+ subscribers; known for detail-heavy "quirks and features" format. |
| SavageGeese | Technical-detail-focused, less entertainment-oriented. |
| Throttle House | High production value, entertainment-oriented. |
| MotorTrend Channel | Video arm of the MotorTrend editorial team. |
| Hagerty | Enthusiast/collector-car focus. |
| carwow | UK-based, includes drag-race-style comparisons. |
| Harry's Garage | UK-based, enthusiast-driver perspective. |
| TFL Truck | Truck/off-road focus. |
| Alex on Autos | Detail-heavy, similar format to Doug DeMuro. |

Video reviews are useful for seeing/hearing the car directly (engine sound, interior fit/finish, infotainment UX) in a way text can't convey, but treat channel personality/style as a factor — cross-check against at least one written source for anything that materially affects a buying decision.

## Safety ratings (not reviews, but commonly wanted alongside them)

| Site | What it provides |
|---|---|
| [IIHS](https://www.iihs.org) | Nonprofit (insurance-industry-funded) crash testing; Good/Acceptable/Marginal/Poor per test, plus Top Safety Pick / Top Safety Pick+ awards. |
| [NHTSA](https://www.nhtsa.gov) | Federal 5-Star Safety Ratings (NCAP) from standardized frontal/side/pole crash tests; also the recall/complaint source already used by the `carfax-analyzer` skill. |

IIHS and NHTSA use different test protocols and can rate the same vehicle differently — check both rather than treating either as definitive.
