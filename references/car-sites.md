# Car search / review site directory

What each major site is actually good for: vehicle search & listings, vehicle reviews, and/or dealership reviews. Compiled from web research; verify against the live site before relying on a feature that may have changed.

## Multi-purpose marketplaces (search + both review types)

| Site | Vehicle search/listings | Vehicle reviews | Dealership reviews |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Cars.com](https://www.cars.com) | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| [CarGurus](https://www.cargurus.com) | Yes — AI-flagged pricing/deal quality | Yes | Yes |
| [Autotrader](https://www.autotrader.com) | Yes — large listing volume, financing marketplace | Yes | Yes |
| [Kelley Blue Book (KBB)](https://www.kbb.com) | Yes — pricing-focused listings | Yes | Yes — via DealerRater integration |
| [TrueCar](https://www.truecar.com) | Yes — price-insight/dealer-offer marketplace | Limited | Yes — certified dealer network reviews |

## Dealership-review specialists

| Site | Vehicle search/listings | Vehicle reviews | Dealership reviews |
|---|---|---|---|
| [DealerRater](https://www.dealerrater.com) | No | No | Yes — dedicated dealership review site, feeds into KBB |

## Vehicle-history / listing aggregators

| Site | Vehicle search/listings | Vehicle reviews | Dealership reviews |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Carfax](https://www.carfax.com) | Yes — listings tied to history reports | No | Limited |
| [iSeeCars](https://www.iseecars.com) | Yes — listing aggregator, AI deal/price analysis | Limited | No |
| [U.S. News Cars](https://cars.usnews.com) | Yes | Yes — aggregates other outlets' reviews into a composite score | No |

## Single-retailer online sellers (own their own inventory)

| Site | Vehicle search/listings | Vehicle reviews | Dealership reviews |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Carvana](https://www.carvana.com) | Yes — own inventory only, fixed pricing | No | N/A — single retailer, not a third-party marketplace |
| [Vroom](https://www.vroom.com) | Yes — own inventory only | No | N/A |
| [CarMax](https://www.carmax.com) | Yes — CarMax inventory only | No | N/A |

Reviews of these retailers themselves (as opposed to "dealership reviews" in the marketplace sense) live on general platforms like BBB/Trustpilot/Google, not an automotive-specific dealer-review site.

|---|---|---|---|
| [Edmunds](https://www.edmunds.com) | Yes | Yes — strongest expert/editorial testing and comparisons | Limited |

## Quick picks by task

- **Searching listings**: Cars.com, CarGurus, Autotrader, TrueCar, KBB.
- **In-depth vehicle reviews before buying**: Edmunds, Car and Driver, MotorTrend, Consumer Reports.
- **Known reliability issues for a model/year**: CarComplaints.com.
- **Checking out the selling dealership's reputation**: DealerRater, or dealer ratings on Cars.com/CarGurus/Autotrader/KBB.
- **Vehicle history**: Carfax (see [[carfax-analyzer]] skill for in-depth report analysis) or AutoCheck.
- **Buying from a single online retailer instead of a dealer**: Carvana, Vroom, CarMax — evaluate the retailer itself via BBB/Trustpilot/Google, not a dealer-review site.
